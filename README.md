# CraveSencee 🍽️

### AI-Powered Food Discovery & Recommendation Platform

CraveSencee is an intelligent food discovery platform designed to make finding the perfect meal easier. It aims to understand users' cravings and provide personalized food recommendations based on preferences, budget, dietary requirements, location and environmental conditions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Simplify the process of discovering food.
- Provide personalized food recommendations.
- Help users find food within their budget.
- Consider dietary preferences and requirements.
- Make food discovery smarter and more convenient.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Smart Food Discovery:** Find food based on natural-language cravings.
- **Personalized Recommendations:** Discover food according to individual preferences.
- **Budget-Based Suggestions:** Explore options that suit your budget.
- **Dietary Preferences:** Consider specific food requirements.
- **Location-Aware Discovery:** Find relevant nearby food options.
- **Restaurant Exploration:** Discover restaurants and available food options.
